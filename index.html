The Perspective Behind My Work

Healthcare, creativity and technology, shaped by a desire to solve meaningful problems.

Hi, I'm Gerald

My tech journey began at Let's Talk Tech. The older I get, the more I notice the blind spots around me, the needs that go unseen and the questions no one asks. Today, I am exploring how 3D and immersive technology can strengthen midwifery education and turn what I notice into practical solutions.

A Healthcare Perspective

Some smiles are carefully curated, hiding stories buried by the fear of judgment. Serving at the bedside as a nurse and midwife reminds me of our shared humanity, and it shapes the problems I choose to solve.

Beyond Technology

I believe innovation should serve people. Every step I take in my social impact journey is purpose driven, and I strongly believe a community thrives when no one is left behind. Anywhere you find a space where persons never have to wonder whether they belong, there you'll find me.
