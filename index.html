Who I am

Chea Gerald comes from the Northwest Region of Cameroon, where his work sits at the intersection of art, health and youth advocacy.

As a nursing and midwifery student, professional artist and founder of Gifted Hands and Healing Voices, he uses creativity as a tool for healing, empowerment, and resilience.

Through community engagements and affiliations with fellowships, such as the Millennium Fellowship, Nivishe Mntal Health Fellowship, Global Arts in Medicine Fellowship, his initiative has been featured in spaces that highlight the power of art in advancing wellbeing and inclusion.

Recognized as a QECT 100 Young Leader Awardee (2026) , Gerald continues to build platfroms that amplify voices, foster safe spaces, and inspire young people to makie healthier choices for their future. In deed, his journey is one of weaving together artistry, healthcare, technology and leadership to transform challenges into opportunities for growth and impact.
